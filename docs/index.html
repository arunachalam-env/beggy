Frequently Asked Questions

What is Beggy?

Beggy is a free anti-spending food delivery simulator for India. It lets you experience the complete thrill of ordering food — browse, add to cart, track the rider on a live GPS map — without spending a single rupee. When the delivery "arrives," the full bill amount is credited to your personal savings vault. It's a behavioral savings tool powered by dopamine redirection.

Does Beggy place real food orders?

No — never. Beggy is a 100% simulation. No real order is ever placed. No delivery partner is dispatched. No restaurant receives any information. The entire experience happens entirely in your browser. Zero real money changes hands.

Is Beggy free to use?

Yes, completely free. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no credit card required. The only optional financial element is voluntarily tipping the student founder via UPI — which you can skip anytime.

How does the dopamine anti-spending trick actually work?

Beggy replicates the full behavioral ritual of ordering food delivery (browsing → selecting → checkout → live tracking). This triggers the same anticipatory dopamine response as a real order. When the 45-second reveal happens — showing the food was never ordered and money was saved — that dopamine reward is redirected toward the savings outcome. Over time, your brain learns to associate craving with saving, not spending.

How does Beggy protect my privacy?

Beggy collects zero personal data. Your savings history lives exclusively in your browser's localStorage and is never transmitted to any server. No account, no email, no phone number is ever required. Vercel edge analytics tracks only anonymous page view counts.

Can I challenge friends to a savings competition?

Yes! After every simulation, Beggy generates a shareable challenge link: https://beggy.vercel.app/?c=340&dish=Biryani&from=YourName. Share it via WhatsApp, X (Twitter), Telegram, LinkedIn, or Instagram. Your friend sees your saved amount and tries to beat it — creating a viral savings challenge loop.

How much money can I realistically save with Beggy?

Regular food delivery orders in India cost ₹300–₹700 per session. If you use Beggy instead of ordering 3–4 times per week, you could save ₹3,600–₹8,400 per month. All amounts are tracked in your anti-spending passbook so you can see your real accumulated savings over time.
